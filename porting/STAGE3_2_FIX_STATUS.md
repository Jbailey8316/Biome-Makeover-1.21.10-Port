# Stage 3.2 Recipe Book Fix

Added vanilla recipe-unlock advancements for all seven crafting-table recipes.

Recipes now unlock when the player obtains the relevant base ingredient:
- Mesmerite unlocks polished Mesmerite and the Mesmerite stairs/slab/wall recipes.
- Polished Mesmerite unlocks its stairs/slab/wall recipes.

Stonecutter recipes and wall tags from Stage 3.1 are retained.
