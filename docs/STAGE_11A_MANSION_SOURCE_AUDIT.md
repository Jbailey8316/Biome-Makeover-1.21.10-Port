# Stage 11A Mansion source/resource/dependency audit

**Mode:** audit only; no Mansion production content is enabled by this
checkpoint. **Authority:** the pinned final Biome Makeover 1.20.1 source and
resource tree under `reference/Biome-Makeover-1.20`.

## Executive result

The released Mansion is an independent custom `biomemakeover:mansion`
structure and `MansionFeature.Piece` implementation. It is not a jigsaw and
does not replace or patch vanilla Woodland Mansion generation. It builds a
three-dimensional room grid, places rotation-aware template pieces, adds wall,
roof, tower and dungeon layers, then interprets directional structure data.
The source tree contains **168** Mansion NBT files, of which **165** are
referenced by the released `mansion.json`; three outer-wall variants are
orphaned. No duplicate SHA-256 groups were found.

Before 11A.1 the current 1.21.10 port contained no Mansion registrations,
templates, Tapestry/Directional Data resources, Red Rose disc, or Mansion
advancement branch. The 11A.1 foundation now adds only the custom structure,
piece, codec, and local grid substrate; all physical/template/marker systems
remain intentionally absent.

## Identity and worldgen contract

* Structure ID/type: `biomemakeover:mansion`, custom `biomemakeover:mansion`
  `StructureType` backed by `MansionFeature.CODEC`.
* Structure set: `biomemakeover:mansions`; weight 1; linear random spread,
  spacing 32, separation 9, salt 420.
* Biomes: `#biomemakeover:has_structure/reworked_mansion`, replaced by the
  `#biomemakeover:dark_forest` tag, whose final value is vanilla
  `minecraft:dark_forest`.
* Generation: `surface_structures`, `beard_box` terrain adaptation. The
  structure computes its start from `WORLD_SURFACE_WG` and lays out pieces
  from the custom codec-supplied template lists. No spawn override is defined
  in the released structure JSON.
* Vanilla relationship: independent structure ID and custom layout; no
  evidence of replacing the vanilla `minecraft:mansion` structure, sharing
  its templates, or changing its placement/frequency.

## Java architecture and helper dependencies

The source classes are `MansionFeature`, `MansionDetails`, `MansionTemplates`,
`MansionTemplateType`, `MansionLayout`, `RoomLayout`, `RoomType`,
`LayoutType`, `TemplateGetter`, room classes (`MansionRoom`,
`NonRoofedMansionRoom`, `RoofMansionRoom`, `BigMansionRoom`, `EntranceRoom`,
`DungeonRoom`, `BossRoom`), and three replacement processors
(`CorridorReplaceProcessor`, `FloorRoomReplaceProcessor`,
`GardenRoomReplaceProcessor`). `MansionFeature.Piece` extends
`TemplateStructurePiece` and serializes `Rotation`, `Ground`, `IsWall`, and
codec-encoded `Details`; it uses separate air/structure-block ignore processors
and a Mansion-specific Beardifier mixin.

`MansionLayout` uses a grid with 12-block X/Z spacing and seven-block floor
spacing. It creates entrance/corridor/room/garden/stair/tower/roof layers,
then a dungeon branch with top/middle/bottom stairs, a corridor, side rooms,
and a terminal boss room. Roof pieces are sorted first; ground pieces carry
terrain adjustment and tower pieces use column rotation. Rotation is selected
from the four cardinal rotations, with connector-aware special cases; mirrors
are not used by the template piece settings.

Final source imports Taniwha `Grid`, `MathUtils`, and `WoodBlockFactory`.
These are helper dependencies, not a reason to add Taniwha to the 1.21.10
runtime. Stage 11 implementation must provide local Grid/random/wood-state
equivalents and modern Structure/Template APIs. The released Directional Data
hook is shared with the structure-piece marker mixin.

## Reference graph and selection

`mansion.json` has four codec groups: `base`, `tower_roof`, `dungeon`, and
`other`. Base groups are corridor straight/corner/T/cross, 34 normal rooms,
11 big rooms, stairs up/down, inner/flat/outer walls, outer windows, seven
gardens and the entrance. Tower/roof groups contain three tower pieces and
the roof 0–4/straight/split variants. Dungeon groups contain seven doors, a
wall, 18 rooms, three stair levels, and the boss room. Other contains the
corner filler and empty template. The Java selectors choose by room type,
grid connectivity, floor, roof topology and random template selection; the
codec lists are not weighted pools. All 165 active references resolve to a
file; only the three listed wall variants are orphaned.

## Markers and structure actions

The `DirectionalData` block/BE marker is removed during placement and its
metadata is dispatched by `MansionFeature.Piece`. Distinct marker families
are:

* `ivy` (19 templates): probabilistic Ancient Oak ivy growth around the
  marker;
* `tapestry` (19): random standing or wall Tapestry variant, orientation-aware;
* `bonemeal` (8): deliberately commented/broken in final source and must stay
  inert;
* `shroom` (1): red/brown/wild mushroom replacement;
* `spawner_spiders` (1): converts a neighboring spawner to Spider or Cave
  Spider;
* `owl` (1, in `boss_room`): creates an Owl with a 25% stunted flag;
* `boss` (1): creates persistent Adjudicator and clears the marker;
* `arena_pos` (1): replaces the marker with Smooth Quartz for boss-room
  arena tracking;
* loot markers: `loot_{standard|common|good|junk|arrow|dungeon|dungeongood|dungeonjunk}`
  with 20/25/40/50/70/75/100 chances and optional replacement block;
* mob markers: `enemy_50`, `ranger_50`, `golem`, `golem_25/50`, `ravager`,
  `cow`, and `allay_50`. Spawn chance is halved by the released handler for
  percentage markers; Allay attempts three times.

Marker ownership crosses the subdivision: marker decoding and template
placement are 11A/11B infrastructure; Tapestries, loot, Owl/mob pools,
Adjudicator and dungeon rewards depend on 11B/Stage 12. The inert `bonemeal`
behavior is historical/dead final behavior, not a missing feature.

## Tapestries, loot, dungeon, Red Rose and advancements

Tapestry is a block plus item family: 16 dye variants and one
`adjudicator_tapestry`, each with standing and wall blocks, shared
`TapestryBlockEntity`, and a client renderer/item renderer. Final source has
no authored crafting recipes. Mansion markers randomly place the 16 ordinary
variants; the Adjudicator drop is boss-owned. Tapestry blocks are structurally
referenced by template palettes, so their Stage 9/11 foundation must precede
template runtime acceptance.

Mansion loot tables are `mansion/standard`, `junk`, `good`, `dungeon`,
`dungeon_good`, `dungeon_junk`, and `arrows`. Directional markers assign the
tables to randomizable containers with source percentages; the complete
tables contain ordinary resources, damaged/enchantable tools, books, and
structure rewards. The `good` and `dungeon_good` tables own
`red_rose_music_disk` (sound `red_rose`, comparator 2, duration 135), not the
Adjudicator entity table. Exact pool entries and probabilities must be ported
from these seven source JSON files in the implementation stage.

Dungeon is not standalone cave worldgen. It is a layout branch connected by
stairs, with seven doors, a wall, 18 room templates and a boss room. The
`boss` marker creates the Stage 12 Adjudicator; `arena_pos` markers define its
arena. Dungeon loot and boss behavior are therefore structurally inseparable
from the Mansion but gameplay-owned by later stages.

Mansion progression includes `mansion` (location), `all_tapestries`,
`enchanted_totem`, and `arm_golem`; the related Dark Forest branch includes
`enter_dark_forest`, `dark_forest_disc`, `crude_cladding`,
`cladding_template`, and `cladded_armor`. The source `mansion` location
predicate uses obsolete singular `structure`; 1.21.10 must use the modern
`structures` representation. `arm_golem` is a custom trigger. Advancement
items depending on Stage 12 must remain deferred rather than weakened.

## Entity and later-stage boundaries

Normal Mansion marker pools use vanilla Vindicators, Evokers, Pillagers,
Ravagers, Cows and Allays, plus the BM Stone Golem pool. Owl markers use the
existing Owl entity. The Adjudicator is persistent and boss-marker-owned;
Adjudicator Mimics and temporary Stone Golem phases are Stage 12 only and
must not gain normal spawn paths. Cladding, dedicated armor, Enchanted Totem,
Red Rose acquisition, Witch quests, Mansion boss systems and Pillager hooks
are later-stage dependencies, not 11A registrations.

## Current-port collision audit

The current production source and packaged JAR contain no `mansion`,
`directional_data`, `tapestry`, `red_rose`, or Adjudicator Mansion
registrations/resources. Existing Ancient Oak, Ivy, Mesmerite, Altar and
other Stage 6/9 foundations are reusable dependencies where their accepted
contracts permit. No Mansion ID is occupied, and no stale Mansion template
path is packaged. This audit made no collision changes.

## Migration-risk map

* **VERY HIGH:** custom Structure/StructureType and StructurePiece codecs;
  1.20.1 settings/serialization, `StructureTemplateManager`, BoundingBox,
  Beardifier and generation-context APIs changed. Strategy: port registry and
  piece codec first, then a packaged one-piece DataFix/load test and a
  `/locate` fresh-world checkpoint before layout expansion.
* **VERY HIGH:** algorithmic grid/dungeon/roof assembly and terrain adjustment;
  errors produce silent missing rooms or floating/buried structures. Strategy:
  deterministic seeded layout snapshots plus physical fresh-world tests.
* **HIGH:** Directional Data marker mixin, marker removal, loot seed assignment,
  entity spawning and client/server separation. Strategy: isolated marker
  contract fixtures and one template per marker family before full generation.
* **HIGH:** Tapestry block/BE renderer and 168 NBT palette IDs; missing valid
  block registrations prevent template loading. Strategy: Stage 9 foundation
  gate, singular-path packaged scan, then DataFix/StructureTemplate load.
* **HIGH:** loot codecs, advancement predicates and custom `arm_golem`
  trigger; modern components and `structures` predicates require semantic
  validation.
* **MEDIUM:** processor and random API signatures, Rotation/Mirror settings,
  structure-set and biome-tag codecs.
* **MEDIUM:** Beardifier mixin and per-piece ground flags; verify against
  current terrain-shaping hooks.
* **LOW:** raw NBT byte preservation, translations and ordinary sound/item
  paths once registries are present.

## Recommended implementation subdivision

1. **11A.1 — Registry and codec foundation:** custom structure/type/piece,
   `MansionDetails`/template codecs, structure set, biome tag, and local Grid /
   random helpers. Milestone: dynamic registries load; no templates active
   yet. Stop on codec/registry errors.
2. **11A.2 — Template migration and marker substrate:** copy the 168 files to
   singular paths unchanged, register Directional Data and Tapestry palette
   dependencies, and add a packaged DataFix/load/hash validator. Milestone:
   every file loads and all 165 references resolve. Stop on unresolved block,
   entity, marker or DataVersion issues.
3. **11A.3 — Physical layout/worldgen:** port grid, room/roof/wall/tower and
   dungeon assembly plus Beardifier/terrain behavior. Milestone: `/locate` and
   a complete fresh-world Mansion with no missing pieces. Stop on layout,
   terrain or existing-world failures.
4. **11B.1 — Marker/loot infrastructure:** Directional Data dispatch,
   container loot seeds, mob pools, Owl/spawner handling and inert bonemeal.
   Milestone: marker fixture templates and loot assignment.
5. **11B.2 — Tapestries/Red Rose:** all standing/wall variants, renderer,
   collection advancement, Red Rose disc and Mansion loot. Milestone:
   obtainable decorations and disc playback.
6. **11B.3 — Dungeon integration:** dungeon loot/doors/arena markers and the
   handoff boundary to Stage 12 Adjudicator. Milestone: complete dungeon
   geometry without activating Stage 12 boss gameplay.

Stage 12 separately owns Adjudicator, Mimics, Stone Golem, Crude/cladding,
Enchanted Totem and dependent progression. Stage 13 owns unrelated Beach and
shared-resource work. No Mythas enhancement is authorized.

## Stage 11A.1 — Mansion registry / codec / structure foundation

**Status:** implemented and locally validated; physical generation remains
deferred to 11A.2/11A.3.

The port now registers `biomemakeover:mansion` as a native 1.21.10
`StructureType` backed by `MansionFeature.CODEC`, plus the matching custom
`MansionFeature.Piece` `StructurePieceType`. The structure codec preserves the
released `settings`, `templates`, and `details` fields using modern
`MapCodec`/`RecordCodecBuilder` APIs. Piece serialization retains the vanilla
template identifier and the released `Rotation`, `Ground`, and `IsWall`
fields through `StructurePieceSerializationContext`; marker actions are not
executed in this stage.

`MansionTemplates` mirrors the released base, tower/roof, dungeon, and other
catalog codec shape without packaging the NBT corpus. `MansionDetails`
preserves the released loot and mob-list codec contract while avoiding any
Stage 12 registrations. `MansionGrid` and `MansionLayoutFoundation` are
narrow local replacements for the Taniwha grid/math helpers: horizontal cell
spacing is 12 blocks, floor spacing is 7 blocks, and random horizontal
selection uses the four cardinal directions. No Taniwha runtime dependency
was added.

The codec and registrations are intentionally inert: no Mansion structure
JSON, structure set, biome injection, or physical NBT templates are active in
11A.1, so incomplete template resources cannot make world generation
reachable. Template migration, DataFix/`StructureTemplate.load`, Directional
Data substrate, marker behavior, and terrain/layout activation are explicit
11A.2/11A.3 work. This preserves the released custom (non-jigsaw) architecture
without placeholder generation.

`Invoke-Stage11A1FoundationValidation.ps1` checks source registrations,
packaged foundation classes, codec/serialization fields, local grid constants,
absence of active Mansion templates/worldgen, absence of marker gameplay, and
Taniwha-free scope. The clean offline build, Stage 10C freeze validator,
integrated parity validator, and `git diff --check` pass. The known offline
dedicated-server `fabric-log4j-util:1.0.2` cache limitation is unchanged.
