# Owl Nest Claim Fix

Changes:
- Adult wild and tamed owls can claim empty nests.
- Search radius increased to 16 blocks.
- Claim scan responds within about one second.
- Claimed state is repaired after reload/block updates.
- Daytime movement uses navigation plus flying move control.
- Close owls are centered on top of the nest to prevent landing beside/below it.
- Sleeping now activates the approved sitting/perch render pose.
- Nest model is a shallower woven bowl with a recessed center.

Test during daytime. Stand more than six blocks away, or sneak while approaching,
because a non-sneaking nearby player intentionally wakes a sleeping owl.
