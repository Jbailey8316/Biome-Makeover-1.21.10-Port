# Future Mythas Enhancement Candidates

These ideas are explicitly outside released Biome Makeover parity and must not be implemented during Stages 0–13
without separate approval.

## Small Lily Pad multi-piece placement

After the parity baseline is frozen, investigate whether player-placed Small Lily Pads should use a modern multi-piece
placement interaction similar in spirit to Pink Petals or Leaf Litter, accumulating approximately one to four pads in
one block space. Preserve the historical models and natural worldgen appearance. This is a Mythas enhancement, not
evidence about released BM behavior.

## Decayed shield AI

After released parity is frozen, investigate making Decayed actively use their visibly carried shields to block
appropriate attacks. Released BM did not implement this defensive AI. It must not be folded into Stage 5 parity.

## Witch Hat and Living World integration

Future Mythas design may connect the Witch Hat and Witch quest to the Mythas Living World system. This is separate
from restoring the released Witch progression and must receive its own approval.

## Scuttler Witch Hats

Future Mythas design may allow Scuttlers to wear Witch Hats. This is custom content, not a released Swamp or Badlands
parity requirement.
